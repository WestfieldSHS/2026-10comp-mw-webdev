Cassie is a seventeen year old girl who grew up in Colorado after her mothers 'death'. She is a talented profiler who can understand people's behaviour and emotions by observing them closely.

cassie joins the FBI's Naturals program to uncover the truth about her mother's disappearance. while in the program she learns to use her natural abilities more while working on cold cases and active cases.

she grows closser to the other teenagers in the program (Dean, Micheal, Lia and Solane) who all have had a hard life but they grow closer throughout the series. Cassie grows especially close to Dean and they eventually they get into a romantic relationship

Dean is a seventeen year old boy who grew up living in a house with his father who is an imfamous serial killer. the hardships from his childhood make dean guarded and reluctant to trust people blindly.

Dean has the natural ability to profile people, especially criminals, understanding the way they think and how they may act. Dean joined the naturals program when he as 12 when his father was aressted.

deans past experiences make him closed off and serious but he eventually becomes closer to the rest of the group

Micheal is a seventeen year old boy who grew up in a wealthy home with his physically and emotionally abusive father. the phyical and emotional abuse from his father led to Micheal developing the ability to read even the most hidden emotions.

Micheal was recruited by Agent Briggs after his wealthy father was indicted for tax fraud, effectively forced into the program to help his father avoid jail time. Micheal uses an arrogant and charming personality to hide his true feelings and vulnerable emotions.

He becomes close to the group and goes through many hardships throughout the series but eventually grows to trust the rest of the naturals
